About · Sisi wenyewe means “we ourselves”

Our name is our conviction: a nation's most sensitive intelligence should be built by its own people, on its own infrastructure, under its own control. Sisi wenyewe.

Sisiwenyewe Tech Uganda develops secure AI systems for defence and national institutions, from intelligence models to the sovereign platforms they run on. Our work is grounded in original research in hyper-heuristics: AI that selects and adapts its own problem-solving strategies, so it stays reliable when conditions change.
